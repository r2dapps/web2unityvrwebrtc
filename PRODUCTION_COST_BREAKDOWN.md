# AetherCare Production Cost Breakdown & Invoice Guide

If you need to provide an invoice or cost breakdown to clients, stakeholders, or hospitals moving from PoC to Production, use this guide. 

The architecture of this application is highly optimized to run almost entirely on **Serverless and Peer-to-Peer (P2P)** infrastructure, meaning your fixed monthly costs are exceptionally low compared to traditional video streaming platforms like Zoom or AWS Kinesis.

## 3. Video Streaming Costs (STUN / TURN)

This is the most critical part to explain to clients. Unlike traditional streaming that sends video to a central server (costing thousands of dollars), WebRTC sends video directly from the Doctor's device to the Patient's device (P2P).

### STUN (Session Traversal Utilities for NAT)
- **Cost:** **$0 (Always Free)**
- **Purpose:** Helps devices find their public IP addresses to connect directly. Google and Cloudflare provide these for free.

### TURN (Metered.ca or Twilio)
TURN is the **Fallback Video Relay Server**. It is ONLY used when a strict firewall blocks the direct P2P connection.

**How Network Types Affect TURN Costs:**
*   **Doctor on Wi-Fi + Patient on Wi-Fi:** 90% chance of a direct P2P connection. Video traffic uses their own ISP bandwidth. **Cost: $0.**
*   **Doctor on Mobile Data (4G/5G) + Patient on strict Hospital Wi-Fi:** High chance that P2P fails due to CGNAT (Mobile) and strict Firewalls (Hospital). Video routes through the TURN server. **Cost: Deducted from TURN data pool.**

**TURN Pricing Breakdown (Metered.ca):**
*   **Free Tier:** 50 GB per month for free.
*   **Video Data Rate:** ~500 MB per hour of HD video.
*   **Free Capacity:** 50 GB gives you roughly **100 hours of relayed fallback video per month** entirely for free.
*   **Paid Tier:** Once you pass 50 GB, Metered charges **$0.40 per GB**.

### Usage Math (PoC vs. Production)

**Scenario 1: Doctor on Wi-Fi + Patient on Wi-Fi (The Ideal Path)**
*   **Usage:** 8-10 hours daily.
*   **Data Routing:** 100% Peer-to-Peer. No TURN server required.
*   **Cost:** **$0.00 / month**.

**Scenario 2: Doctor on Mobile Data (4G/5G) + Patient on Strict Hospital Wi-Fi (The Worst-Case)**
*   **Usage:** 8-10 hours daily (~250 hours / month).
*   **Data Routing:** P2P is blocked. 100% traffic flows through TURN relay.
*   **Data Calculation:** 250 hours * 500 MB = **125 GB / month**.
*   **Cost (PoC Phase):** Metered gives 50 GB free. You pay for 75 GB * $0.40 = **$30.00 / month per active doctor**.

## 4. Hosting Everything Yourself (Self-Hosted PC)

If you have a physical PC or a Dedicated Cloud Server and want to host the TURN relay yourself, here is the cost and setup breakdown:

| Component | Cost | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Physical PC / Dedicated VPS** | **$0 - $20 / mo**| You can use a spare PC in your office, or rent a cheap VPS (e.g., DigitalOcean, Linode). |
| **Electricity & Bandwidth** | **Variable** | Ensure your ISP does not throttle upload speeds. You need at least 10 Mbps UP for smooth HD relaying. |
| **Coturn Software** | **$0 (Open Source)**| You will install `Coturn` (an open-source STUN/TURN server). |

**Setup Guide for Self-Hosting (Coturn + Cloudflare Shield):**
1. Install Linux (Ubuntu) on your PC/VPS.
2. Install Coturn: `sudo apt install coturn`.
3. Configure Coturn to use the **TURN REST API** (this is critical). It allows Coturn to accept time-limited passwords.
4. Set up the Cloudflare Worker (same as before), but point it to your PC's static IP and give the Worker your Coturn REST API secret key.
5. **Security:** The Cloudflare Worker sits safely on the internet. It generates temporary tokens. Hackers cannot ping your PC directly and steal bandwidth without a token generated by Cloudflare.
